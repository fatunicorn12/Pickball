# Opening Cutscenes — ElevenLabs Script

Oct 29, 2026 · @Will Blodgett

Six scenes from waking in the void to the start of gameplay, with the voiced lines ready to paste into ElevenLabs. This replaces the exam cold open and Birdy's intro in the Opening & Tutorial Script.

## How to use

- **Only the grey blocks get voiced.** Each line is `SPEAKER: [tag] line`. Paste a block into ElevenLabs Text to Dialogue and give each speaker a voice, or paste one character's lines at a time into Text to Speech. Leave out the `SPEAKER:` label wherever your tool reads it aloud.
- **Square-bracket tags are ElevenLabs v3-style audio tags**, like \[excited\], \[sighs\], \[laughs\], \[pause\] and \[whispers\]. Generate a line and drop any tag your model doesn't handle well.
- **Italic lines are stage directions** for the cutscene. They aren't voiced.
- **Birdy's secret** is played as a beat too long before certain answers. Those are marked \[pause\], and they should be the only places where his cheer slips.

## Scene 1: The white void

*Pure white. No sound. MC is lying on his back in nothing at all. He sits up slowly.*

```text
MC: [groggy] ...Okay. White void. I'm dead. Probably dead.
MC: [pause] Wait.
MC: [slowly, then building] Wait wait wait. White void. No body pain. Weird calm feeling.
MC: [excited, shouting] Fuck yeah. Isekai!
MC: [eager] Okay. Okay okay okay. Status screen. Status! Open status.
MC: [pause] ...Menu?
MC: [flat] Cool. No menu. Hard mode. Love that for me.
```

*The white swells, then resolves into the courtyard: green lawn, columns, gold sunlight.*

## Scene 2: The lost propeller

*A sputtering whine overhead. Birdy drops out of the sky, bounces off the lawn and scrambles in circles, patting his empty helmet.*

```text
BIRDY: [panicked] No no no no no. Where'd it go? Where did it go?!
BIRDY: [panicked] My propeller! It was right on my head! It's always on my head!
MC: [quietly, to himself] ...What is this guy on about?
BIRDY: [spotting MC, suddenly very close] You! Hi! Sorry. Hi. Emergency.
BIRDY: [pleading] Can you help me find my propeller? Please? I can't fly without it. I can barely stand without it.
MC: [sighs] Okay, okay. I'll help. Where'd you last have it?
BIRDY: [certain] The sky! I had it in the sky!
MC: [flat] That's... not helpful.
BIRDY: [eager] It's round! It spins! It's about this big! [pause] I can't show you how big. My wings are very small.
MC: [sighs] Great. First quest. Fetch a bird his hat.
```

*Gameplay: the platforming section to find the propeller.*

## Scene 3: "You're the one"

*MC holds out the propeller. Birdy snatches it, clicks it onto his helmet, and it whirs to life. He lifts a few inches off the ground, wobbling.*

```text
BIRDY: [gasps, overjoyed] My propeller! You found it! Oh, thank you, thank you, thank you!
BIRDY: [proud] And we're back, baby. One perfect circle, spinning very, very fast.
BIRDY: [pause, staring at MC's back] Wait.
BIRDY: [slowly] Wait wait wait. That sword. That's a compass-blade.
BIRDY: [awed whisper] You're... you're the one. Aren't you?
MC: [confused] The one what?
BIRDY: [excited] The Guardian! I've been looking everywhere for you!
BIRDY: [pause] Well, not everywhere. I crashed. But before I crashed, I was definitely looking.
MC: I have no idea what you're talking about.
BIRDY: [committed] The prophecy! The ancient texts! The...
BIRDY: [pause, losing steam] ...the thing a guy told me about once.
MC: [resigned, under his breath] Is this seriously my fucking companion for this world?
BIRDY: [small, a little hurt] ...I can hear you. I have ears. They're just inside the helmet.
BIRDY: [pause, then bouncing back] But that's okay! First impressions are just rough drafts!
```

## Scene 4: The path

*Jump cut. MC walks a winding stone path between olive trees. Birdy putters along beside him at shoulder height.*

```text
MC: [casual] Okay. Rules. Do I need to eat here? Sleep?
BIRDY: [cheerful] Yes and yes!
MC: Taxes?
BIRDY: [pause] What's a taxes?
MC: [content] God, I love it here.
MC: [casual, testing the waters] So like... do I even need to worry about STDs in here?
BIRDY: [genuinely confused] S-T-D? Is that like... a bird thing?
MC: [beat] Never mind.
```

## Scene 5: The Academy

*The path crests a rise. The whole Academy opens up below: columns, domes, terracotta roofs, the gnomon at the centre.*

```text
BIRDY: [grand, gesturing] And here we are! The Academy!
MC: [awed] Whoa. It's huge.
BIRDY: [chuckles] That's what she said.
MC: [pause, turning to him] Wait. You didn't know what an STD was five minutes ago.
BIRDY: [completely earnest] Oh! No, I have lots of sex. I just... I got them all.
MC: [long pause] ...
BIRDY: [cheerfully] I don't think they're a problem though!
```

*MC stares at him. Birdy beams back. The jokes end here.*

## Scene 6: The world coming apart

*MC's eyes drift north. Past the Academy, the edge of the world is fraying: marble breaks into floating shapes, then ink lines, then blank white. Above it hangs a perfectly black sun.*

```text
MC: [quiet, serious] Hey. What's happening over there?
MC: Why is it... coming apart like that?
BIRDY: [bright, a little too quickly] Oh! That. Yeah. That's the bad part.
BIRDY: [upbeat, like a tour guide] Okay! So. This world has Guardians. Eight of them.
BIRDY: The greatest minds that ever lived. Each one keeps a big idea safe. Angles. Stars. Gravity. That kind of thing.
BIRDY: [pause] But lately, math hasn't been mathing.
BIRDY: [cheerful again] They've all been corrupted by something. And the world's been coming apart at the edges.
BIRDY: [excited] So you, my friend, are going to set them straight!
MC: But how did this even happen? Why are they corrupted?
BIRDY: [pause] I'm... not really sure. It's just been like this for a while.
MC: How long's a while?
BIRDY: [pause, softer] Long enough that people stopped asking.
BIRDY: [bright] But that's why you're here!
MC: Why me, though?
BIRDY: That compass you wield. Only navigators carry those.
BIRDY: [gently] You were a navigator. Somewhere else. Some other world.
MC: [confused] Huh?
BIRDY: [warm] You'll figure it out as you go. Just know that this world needs you.
BIRDY: [pause, then excited] And hey! We're gonna do this together!
```

*Birdy's rotor whirs and he zips ahead down the path. MC looks at the black sun a moment longer, then follows. Gameplay begins.*
